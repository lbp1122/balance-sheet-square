THE SQUARE CONCEPT

A balance sheet made personal.

Businesses use balance sheets to understand financial position. My Wealth Square adapts that idea for individuals: assets sit on one side, liabilities and equity on the other, creating one clear picture of personal wealth.

PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN

Your financial figures stay under your control.

No account is required. Financial figures entered in My Wealth Square are stored locally on your device. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs and does not connect to your bank or investment accounts.
