**Full description**

See your personal balance sheet as one clear, interactive picture.

Balance Sheet Square turns your assets, liabilities and equity into a proportional visual square. Edit any figure and your totals, net worth and key ratios update instantly.

Use it to:

- Record cash, savings, investments, retirement funds, property, vehicles and other assets
- Add home loans, vehicle loans, cards, personal loans and other liabilities
- See net worth and whether assets cover liabilities
- Review debt-to-assets, equity, debt-to-equity and cash-runway ratios
- Explore five editable example scenarios
- Work in English, Bahasa Melayu or Chinese
- Choose RM, USD or CNY
- Save figures locally on your device
- Calculate fully offline
- Export a PDF report and share it using apps you choose

Private by design:

- No sign-in or account required
- No ads or analytics
- Your calculator figures are not collected by the developer
- Financial figures remain on your device unless you choose to export or share a report

Balance Sheet Square is an educational personal-planning calculator. It does not provide financial advice and does not offer loans, banking, trading, investment, insurance or payment services. Legal bankruptcy or insolvency status depends on applicable law and formal proceedings.
