Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

Balance Sheet Square is an educational personal balance sheet calculator developed by LBP. This policy explains how the website and Android app handle information.

Information you enter

The app does not require an account. Asset, liability, expense, language, and currency figures you enter are saved locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, share, or sell those figures.

PDF export and sharing

Reports are generated on your device. A report leaves your device only when you choose a save or share destination. The privacy practices of the destination you select then apply.

Android app and website hosting

The Android app packages the calculator files on the device and does not need an internet connection to perform calculations. The separate public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical request data, such as an IP address, browser type, and device information, to deliver and secure the website under its own privacy terms. Your figures entered into the calculator are not sent to GitHub by Balance Sheet Square.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Balance Sheet Square contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, financial-account connection, or user-tracking service. It does not offer loans, banking, trading, investment, insurance, or payment services.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved calculator figures remain until you clear the app or browser site data, reset your device, or uninstall the Android app. You control any PDF copies you save or share.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the project support page. Do not include personal financial figures in a public support request. A dedicated public support email will also be shown on the Google Play listing when the Android app is released.
